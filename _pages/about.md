Hey! I'm a final-year undergraduate at the Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur, majoring in Statistics and Data Science.

I am currently working with Prof. Soumyarup Sadhukan on Game Theory and with [Prof. Sayak Ray Chowdhury](https://sayakraychowdhury.github.io/) on differentially private alignment of large language models. Our recent preprint, [*Privacy Without Regret: Differentially Private Inference-Time Alignment*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26324), introduces two calibrated-noise mechanisms that add differential privacy to Best-of-N selection with minimal utility loss.

Last summer, I interned as a Product Engineer on the AI team at UnifyApps, Gurugram, where I built AI-workflow tooling and citation rendering for enterprise LLM deployments (I received a PPO). Before that, I worked with Ayush Patnaik at [xKDR Forum](https://xkdr.org/), Bombay, on a satellite-imagery pipeline that quantified subnational economic activity in Russia and Ukraine.

I have also worked on approximate Bayesian inference with Prof. Arnab Hazra, Bayesian cancer trial design with Prof. Dootika Vats, and abstractive text summarization with Prof. Subhajit Dutta — all at IIT Kanpur.

**Research Interests:** Applied Statistics, Game Theory, Sequential Decision Making (Reinforcement Learning), Language Model Optimization.

I am actively seeking remote research or industry internships. If you're building in the agentic AI or applied ML space and are looking for someone with a strong mathematical background, I would love to explore how I can contribute. Please feel free to reach out via email.
