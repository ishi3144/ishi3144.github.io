Course project with Prof. Amit Mitra, IIT Kanpur — Jan 2025 to Apr 2025.

- Predicted next day's high price of Nifty-50 stocks using the past 10 days of open, close, low, volume, and turnover.
- Applied de-trending, fitted ARIMA and GARCH models, and confirmed heteroskedasticity in the dataset using the ARCH test.
- Trained deep learning models including RNN, LSTM, GRU, and attention networks for stock price prediction.
- Achieved best performance with the GRU model, reaching a test MAPE of 0.0064% on unseen data.
