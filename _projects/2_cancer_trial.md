Course project with Prof. Dootika Vats, IIT Kanpur (in collaboration with Johnson & Johnson) — Feb 2025 to Apr 2025.

- Identified clinically relevant mutations from large-scale cancer datasets using prevalence analysis and chi-squared hypothesis testing to evaluate mutation distribution between cancer types.
- Designed survival trial simulations using Kaplan-Meier estimates and log-rank tests with staggered recruitment and dropouts to determine sample size and follow-up needs for statistical power.
- Developed a Beta-Binomial Bayesian interim framework to produce early efficacy estimates and stopping criteria for a trial.
