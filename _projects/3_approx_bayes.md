With Prof. Arnab Hazra, IIT Kanpur — Jan 2025 to Apr 2025.

- Implemented a Bayesian inference technique for problems where the likelihood function is unknown or hard to compute.
- Analyzed limitations of existing methods like Approximate Bayesian Computation (ABC) and Bayesian Synthetic Likelihood (BSL), such as their reliance on thresholds.
- Built an unbiased estimator of approximate likelihood using classification-based discrepancy and Hermite polynomial expansions.
- Compared Expected Evaluation performance with true posterior estimates through simulation experiments under varying MCMC iterations.
